A sweeter way to play DDNet

213Net is a DDNet client with handy lists, visual extras and a few quality-of-life improvements. It adds no cheats and no unfair advantages.

Work in progress. The features below are planned and not released yet. Check the Releases page for the first build.

Planned features

What is coming to 213Net.

Visual enhancements

Rainbow skins, weapon and hook colors, tee trails and smooth animations.

Fonts

A wide selection of fonts, plus support for your own.

Player indicator

Track players around you without zooming out. Not available on PvP servers.

Frozen tee display

See all frozen players on your team.

Info search

Look up player and map data from DDStats.

Warlist

War and team lists, managed with chat commands.

Censor list

Hide or replace unwanted words in chat, nameplates, the scoreboard and more.

Profiles

Save and load skin, colors, emote, name, clan and flag.

Input and ping tools

Reduced input lag and anti-lag ping tools.

Quality-of-life tweaks

Small improvements to the HUD and chat.
