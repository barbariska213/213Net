A DDNet client with custom map backgrounds, handy lists and a few quality-of-life extras.

213Net is an unofficial fork of DDNet. It adds no cheats and no unfair advantages.

Download View on GitHub

Work in progress. The features below are planned and not released yet. Check the Releases page for the first build.

Planned features

Look and feel

  • Custom map backgroundsPick any image from your computer as the background of any map in one click.
  • Visual enhancementsRainbow skins, weapon and hook colors, tee trails and smooth animations.
  • FontsA wide selection of fonts, plus support for your own.

Game info

  • Player indicatorTrack players around you without zooming out. Not available on PvP servers.
  • Frozen tee displaySee all frozen players on your team.
  • Info searchLook up player and map data from DDStats.

Lists and profiles

  • WarlistManaged with chat commands, with separate war and team lists.
  • Censor listHide or replace unwanted words in chat, nameplates, the scoreboard and more.
  • ProfilesSave and load skin, colors, emote, name, clan and flag.

Connection and extras

  • Discord Rich PresenceShows your friends that you are playing 213Net.
  • Input and ping toolsReduced input lag and anti-lag ping tools.
  • Quality-of-life tweaksSmall improvements to the HUD and chat.
